01 / Dataset

This visualization reuses the instructor-provided dataset of the 50 largest economies by 2025 nominal GDP, measured in billions of current U.S. dollars. Countries outside the dataset are shown as missing data, not zero GDP.

02 / Voice Control

Click Start Voice Control, allow microphone access, and speak one English command. Click Start again for your next command.

  • “Highlight China” — highlight China in both views.
  • “Highlight Japan” — display Japan’s GDP and rank.
  • “Linear scale” — use a linear color scale.
  • “Log scale” — use a logarithmic color scale.
  • “Reset” — clear selection and reset map zoom.

“Select” and “Show” also work before a country name. Use names from the country dropdown; “USA” and “UK” are supported aliases.

05 / Design Reflection

I extended my Lab 9 GDP choropleth and Dorling cartogram with voice interaction using the Web Speech API. Users can say “Highlight China” or “Highlight Japan” to select an economy across both views and display its GDP and rank. They can also change the color scale or reset the visualization. These commands support locating named economies and exploring differences in economic magnitude. Voice is appropriate because country names provide clear targets, especially for countries that are difficult to click on a world map. The interface displays the recognized transcript and resulting action. Listening starts only after an explicit button click and can be stopped. Mouse, keyboard, dropdown, button, and typed-command controls remain available as fallbacks. Live microphone recognition still requires testing on the user’s device. Potential limitations include accents, background noise, country-name transcription errors, browser compatibility, and network-dependent recognition. Unknown commands leave the visualization unchanged and display instructions for trying again.
