Cities Multivariate Data

Design Justification

I used position, size, and color to represent the four variables in the dataset. Population is mapped to the x-position. Temperature is mapped to the y-position. Development level is represented by circle size, with larger circles indicating higher development levels. Region is represented by color so that cities from the same region can be quickly identified. City names are shown as labels, and tooltips provide additional information when the viewer moves over a circle.
