Temporal Network Observations

1. How does overall network connectivity change across the 60 days?

Overall network connectivity increases over time. Early in the 60-day period, the network consists of several small or separate components. As new relationships appear, these clusters become increasingly connected, resulting in a more interconnected network later in the period.

2. Which companies become more central or commercially active over time?

Fusion Electronics (c06) becomes increasingly central and commercially active over time, developing additional relationships with several other companies. Ion Systems (c09) and Cedar Retail (c03) also become more active later in the period, particularly through cross-regional transactions.

3. Are there periods when the network consists of relatively separate clusters?

Yes. During the early period, the network contains relatively separate clusters. These clusters become increasingly connected as new commercial relationships appear between companies from different parts of the network.

4. Which commercial relationships appear, disappear, or become stronger over time?

Several relationships involving Fusion Electronics (c06) become more prominent over time, including c06–c07, c06–c08, c06–c10, and c06–c12. Additional relationships involving c03 and c09 also appear later, contributing to a denser commercial network.

5. Does the network become more or less cross-regional over time?

The network becomes more cross-regional over time. Later days contain more relationships connecting companies across Asia, Europe, and North America, making the network increasingly geographically integrated.
